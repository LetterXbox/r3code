# R3 Code privacy policy — draft

This draft needs review before publication. It describes R3 Code as a client, and does not replace the privacy policies of services you connect to it.

R3 Code handles the information you enter or open in the app, which can include conversations, prompts, files, images, project paths, terminal output, settings and account information. The app and its connected environment may store this information locally. Settings and connection credentials may be retained so that the app can restore them.

When you connect a provider, remote environment, T3 Connect or another service, R3 Code and its connected environment send the information needed to perform the actions you request to that service. The service's own privacy policy and retention practices apply. For example, a prompt or attachment sent to an agent may be processed by the selected provider. Opening a web page can send information to that page's operator.

The app can access files, microphone, camera, screen capture and other system resources when you choose a feature that uses them and macOS grants access. You can review or change operating-system permissions in macOS System Settings.

Diagnostics, logs and exported telemetry may contain technical information and, depending on their contents and configuration, sensitive data. Review them before sharing. Connected environments can have their own logging, telemetry and retention settings.

You can manage local settings and data through the app and your operating system, and account or remote data through the relevant service. Removing the app alone may not remove data held by connected services or all local application data.

Before publishing: confirm the data controller and contact address; audit storage locations, retention, analytics and network destinations; document deletion and access requests and any applicable jurisdiction-specific rights; and obtain appropriate review. The separate T3 Code privacy policy is available at https://t3.codes/privacy-policy.
